# GIG Roster

GIG (God is Good) Roster is a web app for organising church worship teams and their schedules. It gives members and team leaders a shared place to manage assignments, availability, and upcoming events.

## Open the app

**[Open GIG Roster on GitHub Pages](https://ckangnz.github.io/GIG_Roster/)**

1. Open the link above in your browser.
2. Select **Login with Google** and sign in with your Google account.
3. Complete your profile, then join an existing organisation or create one. You can also join through an invitation link from your organisation.
4. If the organisation requires approval, wait for an administrator to approve your membership. Your administrator can assign you to teams and positions.

## What you can do

- View upcoming events and your roster assignments on the dashboard.
- Browse rosters by team, member, or position.
- Manage assignments, mark absences, and claim available shifts.
- Add upcoming events to your calendar.
- Share thoughts with your team and react to others' posts.
- Manage organisations, teams, members, and positions as an administrator.
- Use the app in English or Korean, with light and dark themes.

## About the project

Built with React, TypeScript, Vite, Redux Toolkit, and Firebase. The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages, with Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication and shared data stored in Cloud Firestore.

[View the source code on GitHub](https://github.com/ckangnz/GIG_Roster).
